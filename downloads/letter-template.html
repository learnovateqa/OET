GreetingDear Ms Carter, · Dear Dr Khan, · Dear Charge Nurse, · Dear Sir/Madam,
1 · Purpose (1–2 sentences)I am writing to refer / inform you that / request … patient, a age-year-old man/woman with condition, for / who requires what you want.
2 · Background and history (relevant only)Diagnosis, key dates, treatment so far, important history and medication. Summarise long histories; group similar notes.
3 · Current situationHow the patient is now: findings, needs, concerns, social situation that affects care.
4 · Requests (purpose expanded)I would be grateful if you could … · Could you please … · He / She will need …
5 · Closing linePlease do not hesitate to contact me if you require any further information.
Closing and roleYours sincerely, (named reader) · Yours faithfully, (no name)

Registered Nurse
Ward / Clinic name

Before you write (5 minutes)

  • Who is the reader? Do they know the patient already?
  • What will the reader do for the patient?
  • Tick what they need; cross out what they do not.
  • Chronological, or by theme (most important first)?

Purpose verbs

  • refer · request · transfer · discharge
  • update · inform · advise · thank you for accepting

Genre and style

AvoidUse

Thanks for having a lookThank you for seeing …

Useful links

  • He was admitted on … with …
  • Despite …, his … has deteriorated.
  • On examination, … · Since then, …
  • Of note, … · In addition, …
  • Please note that she is allergic to …
Final 3-minute check
  • Purpose clear in the first paragraph?
  • Every fact correct, in the right tense?
  • Anything the reader does not need?
  • Layout: date, Re:, greeting and closing match?
  • Body about 180–200 words?
