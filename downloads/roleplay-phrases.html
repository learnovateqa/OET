Role-play phrases for nurses

OET Prep Course · Elevate with Ms. Maria · Learnovate Qatar. 3 minutes to prepare, about 5 minutes to speak. Use different phrases in each role-play, and change the words to fit the patient.

Plan in 3 minutes

  • Who is the patient? How might they feel?
  • How will I start? (setting: first meeting, after an examination, emergency?)
  • One open question for each “find out” task
  • Two or three lay words for each medical term
  • How will I check understanding?

Opening

  • Good morning. I’m one of the nurses here. Could you tell me your name, please?
  • Thank you for coming in. What would you like to talk about today?
  • I understand you’re going home today. Is it OK if we go through a few things first?

Gathering information

  • Can you tell me a bit more about …?
  • How has that been affecting you?
  • When you say “a bit”, roughly how many …?
  • So, from what you’ve told me, … Is that right?

Empathy

  • I can see this has been a really worrying time for you.
  • That sounds very frustrating. I’m sorry you’ve had that experience.
  • It’s completely understandable to feel nervous about this.
  • You’re not alone in feeling that way.

Explaining

  • Can I ask what you already know about …?
  • Let me explain what this means in simple terms.
  • There are three main things to remember. The first is …
  • It works a bit like …
  • The most important thing is …

Checking understanding

  • Just so I know I’ve explained it clearly, could you tell me how you’ll …?
  • Does that make sense so far?
  • How do you feel about what I’ve suggested?
  • What questions do you have about that?

Structure and signposting

  • Now, I’d like to talk about …, if that’s OK.
  • Before we move on, …
  • Is it OK if we come back to that in a moment?
  • So, to sum up, we’ve agreed that …

Difficult moments

  • I’m sorry that this has happened. Let me tell you what we know.
  • I’m afraid the results aren’t what we hoped. Would you like me to go through them now?
  • I respect that it’s your decision. Can I explain why we recommend …?
  • What would help you feel more comfortable with this?

Closing

  • Is there anything else you’d like to ask before you go?
  • Here is a leaflet with this information to take home.
  • If you have any problems, please call us on the number on the leaflet.
  • Thank you for talking with me today. Take care.
